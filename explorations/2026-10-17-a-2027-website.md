# A 2027 website

*An exploration, started 17 October 2026, nothing decided yet. Kyle's words
are quoted as his; everything else is Claude's reading and proposal.*

## The example

Kyle sent [OpenCode 2.0](https://anoma.ly/notes/opencode-2-0/), Anomaly's
release note, as "a great example" of what this site's 2027 premise is.

What the page does, read from its source and in Chrome on 2026-10-17:

- **Book-set prose.** Valkyrie, justified, with real hyphenation. The line
  breaks are worked out at build time and written into static HTML: every
  word, space and hyphen is its own element, about 800 of them. The page
  declares itself `"mode":"static"` and reads with nothing loaded on top.
- **A room for an opening.** A dark 3D studio with a cube toggle for the 3D
  and a speaker toggle for sound. A red hand-drawn note writes itself onto it.
- **Replays, not videos.** Each demo is the product's real interface, typed
  and clicked again in front of you after you press play.
- **What it costs.** The HTML is 850 KB, it opens on a loading bar, and it is
  dark only. It was not checked at phone width.

## The premise, as Claude reads it

A 2027 website is built for one job and finished all the way down. It has the
kind of detail and features that used to take a studio of specialists and now
take one person working with an assistant. Take two things from the example,
**replays** and **typesetting**, and never its look.

For this site, the meticulous detail can come from data nobody else has: the
archive, daybook's captures and transcripts, the weather record, sounding,
and the prints model.

## Directions

### Replays

- **A painting replayed.** Its making played back from daybook's captures at
  the moments marked in them, with Kyle's words from the transcripts set
  alongside, marked as his.
- **A day replayed.** The day a work was made: its weather from lab/weather,
  the light, the clips, and sounding's score for that day.
- **A print replayed.** Plate by plate and state by state, from the prints
  model (oeuvre `docs/prints-spec.md`).

### Typesetting as a craft, not a font choice

- Centurion (the Coelacanth fork), justified, with hyphenation worked out at
  build time.
- Real small caps, hanging punctuation, and notes in the margin instead of
  footnotes.
- Each page composed the way a forme is composed, and printable as a proper
  sheet.
- The first thing to test: how build-time line breaks behave at phone width.

### Small details only this data allows

- Where each work was made, which is the Territory sketch (site PR #6).
- What Kyle said about it, marked as his words.
- What was happening in the studio that week.

## Open

- **What is the site's job?** "Purpose built for a task" needs the task
  named. Three candidates: watching the 1000 being made, reading the body of
  work like a book, or the storefront for the films. Each puts different
  features first.
- **The first prototype**, proposed and not yet agreed: one work from the
  spring 2024 slice (The Memory of Atmosphere, daybook's foundation plan),
  set in Centurion, with a replay of its making from daybook, as a working
  page in Chrome.
