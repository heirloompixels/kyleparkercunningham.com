Deep time,
read downward.

He tags fourteen works deep time and paints trees older than the state. So: read the practice the way you read a road cut. Bed thickness is the number of works deposited that year. Bed colour is the average measured hue of everything in it. The hatched beds are unconformities — years where nothing was laid down at all.

Reading the column

bed colour = mean hue
unconformity — no deposition

Thickness is honest: one work is one unit. The 2018 and 2021 beds really are twenty-five times thicker than 2009.

Why a column and not a timeline

A timeline spaces years evenly and so tells you nothing. A column gives the years their real weight, and makes the empty ones visible as gaps rather than as blank space you scroll past.

The unconformities

2010 and 2011 hold nothing. That is not an error in the catalogue — see sheet 47, which is about exactly those years and argues they should stay visible.
