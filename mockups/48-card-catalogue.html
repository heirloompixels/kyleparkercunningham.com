Card catalogue

The archive is a database and behaves like one. Before databases this is what an archive was: a rod through the bottom of a stack of cards, one card per object, typed once and never reformatted. Every field on these cards is real and comes straight from the record. The point is that a card is finite — it can hold about forty words, so somebody has to decide which forty.
