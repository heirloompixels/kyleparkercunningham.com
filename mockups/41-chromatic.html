Kyle Parker Cunningham · chromatic index

Sorted by colour, not by year.

Every photograph in the archive carries a measured dominant hue and a five-colour palette. Order the whole catalogue by that instead of by date and a different practice appears: one continuous spectrum, heavy in ochre and teal, thin in violet, with the recent weather paintings all arriving together in the blues. Height is saturation. Hover anything to see the work underneath its own colours.

redochregoldgreentealbluevioletneutral

155 works. Hue is the saturation-weighted circular mean of each photograph; near-neutral works (chroma below 0.06) can't be meaningfully placed on a colour wheel, so they sit at the end ordered by lightness instead of pretending to a hue they don't have.
