It is dense, ugly, entirely numeric, and astronomers have preferred it to every prettier alternative for four hundred years — because you can find one line in it in a second. Magnitude

Borrowed honestly from astronomy and computed the same way: −2.5 log₁₀ of the motif's mean area in cm², so smaller numbers are bigger objects. It is a joke that happens to work.

Max gap

The longest the motif has been absent between two appearances. The largest values in the table are the interesting ones — those are the things he came back to after years of not.

Why this and not a grid

Because a grid of 155 thumbnails answers 'what does it look like' and nothing else. This answers 'when', 'how often', and 'is it due back' — and fits on one screen.
