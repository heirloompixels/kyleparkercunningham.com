A Field Guide to the

Fauna of the Near Future

Being an account of the creatures observed in the paintings, prints and found objects of Kyle Parker Cunningham, 2014–2023, with their apparatus, habitats and colouration

XXII Plates · XXVI Specimens Field-guide apparatus compiled by Owl, the studio’s machine. The works and their titles are the artist’s.

How to use this guide

  1. Look. Each plate shows one species as the artist recorded it, in paint, in drypoint, or on found wood. Some were recorded twice, years apart; those plates carry more than one specimen.
  2. Sight it. Linger on a plate, or tap Record sighting, and it is stamped into your life list at the foot of the page.
  3. Mind the apparatus. Most of these animals wear something: a dish, a helmet, a respirator, a rocket. The table says what, taken from the record of each work.
  4. Take a card. Any plate will print you a specimen card to keep or send. Collect all twenty-two and the guide says so.

    Your life list

    This guide covers the creatures alone: twenty-two species, recorded between 2014 and 2023. It leaves out the clouds, the self-portraits, the still lifes, the abstractions, the atmospheres of 2024 and everything since.

    New species are expected. From the winter solstice, 21 December 2026, the artist begins a thousand new works in a year.
