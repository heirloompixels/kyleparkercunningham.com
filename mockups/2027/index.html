Ten sites for 2027

The archive keeps everything. The site gets a stranger's few minutes, and has to show what Kyle is doing now and the best of what came before. Here are ten ways to spend those minutes, each built far from the others on purpose. Every one is a working prototype using the real catalogue: 159 works, their real sizes and colours, and Kyle's own spoken words.

Each design ends at the archive. Nothing here uses a price, and no new words are written as Kyle; where a machine speaks, it signs as Owl. Anything that doesn't exist yet (the 1000's progress, today's clip, where he is now) is marked as an example. One line, in The Reel and Ten Miles, comes from something Kyle typed in a private session on 27 September; it stays out of anything public until he says so.

How they combine

These are sketches, not candidates for one winner. My read on which parts belong together:

  1. The spine is Today and The Thousand. They are one idea at two scales. The front page is today's sky, today's work and today's line, and the field of 1000 is the same year seen from above. From the solstice, they're the reason to come back tomorrow.
  2. Owl replaces the menu. A visitor who wants something specific asks, and gets three works and a door into the archive. The same layer answers agents, with structured data on every work.
  3. The Reel is how the site leaves the site. Each panel is already a post. It belongs in reception's queue as much as on the homepage.
  4. Channel is the films' home, once the films have a public host. Until then it's a placeholder broadcast, and it says so.
  5. True Size is a button, not a site. "See it at its real size" on every work, in the archive as well.
  6. Calm, Fauna and Ten Miles are artworks in their own right. Each is small and self-contained, so they fit the portals house better than the homepage. Each is also a launch moment: one release, one link people pass around.
  7. Territory is the "where" room, and it fills itself as the 1000 is made on the road. It needs places recorded: 149 of 159 public works have none today.

The full list of what has to happen first, beginning with what needs Kyle, is the review.
